❓Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a profile?

Open the app and follow the on-screen instructions. You'll need to enter your child's name, select their age, choose their preferred language, and optionally pick favorite numbers.

What age is this app suitable for?

The app is designed for children ages 5-10 who are learning basic addition and subtraction with number bonds.

How does the adaptive learning work?

The app tracks your child's performance (accuracy, speed, and consistency) and automatically adjusts problem difficulty. Children must demonstrate mastery (90%+ accuracy over 10 problems) before advancing to the next level.

Can I change the language?

Yes! You can select English, Spanish (Español), or French (Français) when creating a profile or from the profile settings.

What is the "crossing strategy"?

The crossing strategy is a mental math technique where numbers are broken down to cross tens boundaries. For example, 9 + 7 becomes 9 + 1 + 6 (reaching 10 first, then adding the remainder). This builds strong number sense.

How do I reset my child's progress?

Go to the Profile screen and tap the reset button. Note that this will clear all progress and cannot be undone.

Are there any in-app purchases or ads?

No! Number Bond Challenge is completely free with no ads and no in-app purchases.
