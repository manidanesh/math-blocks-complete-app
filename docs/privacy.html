🔒 Privacy Policy

Number Bond Challenge

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Number Bond Challenge is a math learning app designed for children. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting all user data.

1. Data Collection

The app stores profile information locally on your device, including your child's name, birth year, language, and preferences. Birth year is used to calculate an estimated age as the current year minus birth year. The app does not ask for a birth month or day.

  • No online account or email address is required.
  • All progress, scores, and preferences are stored locally on your device only.
  • Older profiles retain their previously saved age until you provide a birth year.

2. Data Sharing

We do not share any information with third parties.

  • The app does not include analytics, advertising, or social media links.

3. In-App Purchases & Ads

The app does not include in-app purchases or third-party advertising.

4. Children's Privacy

This app is built specifically for children under 13, in compliance with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act).

  • Parents can delete the app at any time to remove all stored data.
