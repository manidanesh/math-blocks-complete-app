Your Privacy is Our Priority: Number Bond Challenge does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information stays on your device.

Information We DON'T Collect

Number Bond Challenge is designed with privacy first. We do NOT collect:

  • Personal information (names, emails, phone numbers)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or statistics
  • Crash reports with personal data
  • Any data that leaves your device

Local Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Child Profile: Name, age, language preference, and favorite numbers
  • Progress Data: Problem attempts, accuracy, and performance metrics
  • Rewards: Stars earned and current level

This data is saved using iOS's secure local storage (SharedPreferences) and is never transmitted to any server or third party.

Third-Party Services

We do NOT use any third-party services that collect data, including:

  • No analytics platforms (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
  • No advertising networks
  • No crash reporting services with personal data
  • No social media integration
  • No cloud storage or backup services

Internet Connection

Number Bond Challenge does not require an internet connection to function. The app works completely offline after installation. No data is sent to or received from any server.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We are committed to protecting children's privacy:

  • The app is designed for children under 13
  • We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children
  • No registration or account creation is required
  • Parents have full control over all data through device settings
  • All data can be deleted by uninstalling the app

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your data is protected by your device's security measures (passcode, Face ID, etc.)
  • Data is isolated to the app's sandbox environment
  • No data transmission means no risk of interception
  • Deleting the app permanently removes all data

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All data is accessible within the app
  • Deletion: Uninstall the app to permanently delete all data
  • Control: Modify or reset profiles at any time
  • Export: Data stays on your device, no need for export

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
